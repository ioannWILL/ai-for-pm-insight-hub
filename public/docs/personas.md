# Personas

Who you interviewed, what they're trying to get done, and **how much evidence backs each persona**.

## What's on the card

- **Tagline & description** — a grounded summary, built from the quotes, not invented.
- **Evidence level** — `strong`, `moderate` or `thin`. A thin persona (one interviewee, or mostly other people talking about them) says so, out loud, with a note explaining why.
- **Goals & frustrations** — the recurring ones.
- **Jobs to be done** — phrased *"When …, I want to …, so I can …"*, with an importance rating.
- **Interviewees** — who represents this persona, and how many tagged quotes they contributed.

## Open the evidence

Click any **job** to open the drawer and read the quotes **for** (▲) and **against** (▼) it. That's the core move everywhere on the board: a claim is only as good as the quotes behind it, and the drawer shows both sides.

## Filtering

Use the **Persona** filter in the top bar to focus the whole board on one persona. The Personas tab then shows just that card.

## A note on honesty

Insight Hub is built to make thin evidence visible, not to hide it. If a persona rests on a single interview, the board flags it — treat those as hypotheses to test with the next round, not settled fact.
