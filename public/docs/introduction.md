# Introduction

**Insight Hub** turns a folder of customer-interview transcripts into a living product-research board: **personas**, **journey maps**, a **RICE-ranked feature list**, a **delivery backlog**, and an **ask-the-interviews** panel — with every statement linked back to the exact quotes for and against it.

Claude does the reading. You review and decide.

## Why it exists

Research dies in documents. You run a dozen interviews, write them up, and three weeks later nobody remembers which persona needed what, or why a feature is on the roadmap. Insight Hub keeps the thread: every persona, pain, hypothesis and feature stays wired to the verbatim quotes behind it, so you can always answer *"who said this, and did anyone disagree?"*

## What it is not

- **Not a recorder or transcriber.** Bring your own transcripts (any tool, any language).
- **Not a cloud service.** It runs on your computer. The only thing that leaves your machine is the transcript text sent to Claude to process it.
- **Not a black box.** Every step writes plain JSON to a folder you choose, and every claim on the board opens the quotes behind it.
