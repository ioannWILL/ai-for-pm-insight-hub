# Ask the interviews

A chat panel that answers questions about your research — **only from the tagged quotes**, with citations.

## How it answers

- It searches the tagged quotes and answers from them, **citing the quote ID** behind every claim. Click a citation to read the quote.
- It points out **disagreement** between interviewees and flags when evidence is thin or comes from one person.
- If the interviews **don't** contain the answer, it says so, rather than guessing. It won't pad an answer with loosely related quotes or fall back to general knowledge.

## Going beyond the interviews

Sometimes you *want* an outside view. Say so explicitly in your question — for example *"look beyond the interviews"* or *"what does the wider market say?"* — and it will add a clearly-labelled section of general knowledge, **after** giving what the interviews say. It never presents outside knowledge as something an interviewee said.

## Examples

- *"Which pains came up most often, and from whom?"*
- *"Where do interviewees disagree with each other?"*
- *"What are people already paying for today?"*
- *"Which top-5 RICE features have the weakest evidence?"*

## It runs on your Claude connection

Answers use the same Anthropic API key or Claude Code connection as the rest of the app (see [Connecting Claude](#docs/claude)). Nothing is sent anywhere else, and only the tagged quotes relevant to your question are included.
