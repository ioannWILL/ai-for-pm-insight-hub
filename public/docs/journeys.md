# Journey maps

One map per persona. Each **column** is a step of their journey, in time order; each **row** is a lens on that step.

## Rows

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Pain points** | What hurts at this step. |
| **Jobs to be done** | What they're trying to accomplish here. |
| **Existing tools & workarounds** | What they use today, named. |
| **Flow with our product** | How the step works with your product now — or the gap, if it doesn't. |
| **Features to implement** | What to build here. Green cards link to a feature in the [RICE](#docs/rice) list. |

## Reading it

- The **emotion curve** across the top is the overall feeling at each step, from the evidence.
- Every statement shows a **▲ for / ▼ against** tally. Click one to read the quotes.
- A **green feature card** shows its live RICE score and status, and links straight to the feature.

## Switching personas

Use the persona chips on the right of the view header, or the global **Persona** filter. The journey redraws for the selected persona.

## Empty cells

A cell marked *"No evidence yet"* is a genuine blind spot — no quote covered that step and lens. Those gaps are useful: they tell you what to ask in the next interview. See [Evidence & coverage](#docs/evidence).
