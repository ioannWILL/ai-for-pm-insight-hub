# Evidence & coverage

Where the evidence is thick, and where you're guessing.

## The heatmap

A grid of **tagged quotes per journey step**, per persona. Dark cells are well-covered; pale or empty cells are **blind spots** — steps nobody talked about. Click a cell to read every quote for that step.

Use it to plan the next round of interviews: aim at the pale cells.

## Quote types

The strip at the top counts quotes by type — pain, job, need, workaround, tool, feature request, willingness-to-pay, praise, objection — for the current filter. A board that's all `pain` and no `wtp` tells you something about what you still need to learn.

## Hypotheses

Each hypothesis from the taxonomy is scored **for vs against**, from the quotes tagged to it. Click one to read both sides. A hypothesis with lots of *against* is doing its job: it's telling you the idea is contested.

## Sources

The full list of interviews: date, interviewee, role and company, persona, type, quote count, and the source file. Click the quote count to read everything one person said.

## The point

This view exists to keep you honest. It's easy to over-index on the loudest interview; the heatmap and the for/against tallies make the shape of your evidence — and its gaps — impossible to miss.
